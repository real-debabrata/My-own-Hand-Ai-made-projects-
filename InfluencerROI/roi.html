What is COGS?

Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) refers to the direct costs of producing the goods sold by a company.

  • Cost of materials (Fabric, ingredients, plastic)
  • Direct labor costs to manufacture the item
  • Packaging costs per unit
Tip: Lowering COGS increases your profit margin without needing to raise prices.

Influencer Strategy

Influencer Marketing isn't just advertising; it's borrowing trust. You are paying to access an audience that already trusts the creator.

  • Reach: How many people see the post.
  • Engagement: Likes/Comments indicate interest.
  • Conversion: The % of people who actually buy.
Tip: Micro-influencers (10k-50k followers) often have higher conversion rates than celebrities.

The Psychology

Why does influencer marketing generate sales? It relies on Social Proof and Parasocial Relationships.

  • Authenticity: It feels like a recommendation from a friend, not a faceless brand.
  • Niche Targeting: You reach specific interest groups (e.g., "Tech lovers" or "Yoga moms") instantly.
Tip: Video content (Reels/TikTok) usually converts better than static images.
